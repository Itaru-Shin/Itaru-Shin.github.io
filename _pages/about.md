I am a PhD student in Computer Science at North Carolina State University. I joined NC State in 2023 and have been advised by [Frank Mueller](https://arcb.csc.ncsu.edu/~mueller/) and [Yuan Liu](https://yuanliu.group/) since 2025. My research focuses on quantum computing, specifically quantum error correction, quantum state preparation, and quantum algorithms for hybrid continuous-variable and discrete-variable systems.

Before joining NC State, I received my Ph.D. in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics (2017 to 2023) and my B.S. in Energy and Power Engineering (2013 to 2017), both from Xi'an Jiaotong University, where my research was in computational fluid dynamics (CFD).

## Research interests

- Quantum error correction for hybrid continuous-variable and discrete-variable systems
- Quantum information processing
- Quantum algorithms
